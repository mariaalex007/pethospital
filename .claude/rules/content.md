# Content and compliance

## Voice

- Warm, simple, reassuring English. Short sentences. Speak to the pet owner ("your pet").
- Avoid jargon; explain medical terms in plain words.
- Every page ends with a clear next step: Book Appointment, WhatsApp or Call.
- Tamil content only if the client confirms a bilingual site.

## Facts

- Never invent doctor names, qualifications, years of experience, prices, timings, equipment, awards or statistics. Use `[TBC: ...]` placeholders.
- No medical guarantees ("100% cure", "painless"). Describe what the clinic does, not promised outcomes.
- Emergency page/section must say clearly what to do and the emergency number, and not give at-home treatment instructions that could delay care.

## Reviews and testimonials

- Use only real reviews (Google reviews or written ones the client provides with the reviewer's permission). Never write or paraphrase fake reviews.

## Photos

- Patient and owner photos only with the owner's permission.

## Legal pages

- Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy are drafts until the client (or their adviser) approves them. Mark them `DRAFT — for client review`.
- Privacy Policy must cover what the appointment form collects, why, how long it is kept, WhatsApp/email sharing, cookies/analytics, payment processing by Razorpay, and how to request deletion, in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.
- Forms include a consent checkbox linking to the Privacy Policy.

## Drafting workflow

- Draft copy in `docs/content/<slug>.md` with front matter: `title`, `meta_description`, `h1`, `primary_keyword`.
- Client reviews the Markdown, then it is moved into WordPress.
