# Project brief — Vetri Pet Clinic website

Source: quotation sent to the client.

## Design & development features

- 13 to 15 pages: Home, About Us, Contact Us, Reviews, Gallery, 6 Service Pages, Terms & Conditions, Privacy Policy
- Premium WordPress theme, plugins and custom designs
- Premium colours and mobile-responsive design
- Social media integration
- WhatsApp sidebar and appointment form integration
- Appointment details sent to the clinic's WhatsApp
- Payment gateway with UPI options
- Google Business Profile (GMB) page integration
- Basic SEO and website testing
